## What is the capital of Australia?

**Canberra**, not Sydney or Melbourne.

## What is the capital of Canada?

**Ottawa**, on the border between Ontario and Quebec.

## What is the capital of Brazil?

**Brasília**. It was built as a new capital and opened in 1960. Rio de Janeiro was the capital before it.

## What is the capital of Turkey?

**Ankara**, not Istanbul, which is the largest city.

## What is the capital of Switzerland?

**Bern**.

## What is the capital of Nigeria?

**Abuja**. It replaced Lagos as the capital in 1991.

## What is the capital of New Zealand?

**Wellington**, at the southern tip of the North Island.

## What is the capital of Kazakhstan?

**Astana**.

## What is the capital of Myanmar?

**Naypyidaw**. It replaced Yangon as the capital in 2006.

## What is the capital of Morocco?

**Rabat**, not Casablanca, which is the largest city.

## What is the capital of Vietnam?

**Hanoi**, in the north. Ho Chi Minh City is the largest city.

## What is the capital of Argentina?

**Buenos Aires**.

## What is the capital of India?

**New Delhi**.

## What is the capital of Tanzania?

**Dodoma**. Dar es Salaam is the largest city.

## What are the capitals of South Africa?

South Africa has three:

- **Pretoria**: the government (executive)
- **Cape Town**: the parliament (legislative)
- **Bloemfontein**: the courts (judicial)

## What are the capitals of Bolivia?

- **Sucre**: the constitutional capital
- **La Paz**: where the government sits
