### Added

- Decks are plain Markdown files. Write one by hand or ask any AI to write one, then import it.
- Import a deck from a file or by pasting text. A preview shows the cards, any problems and which images are missing before anything is saved.
- Review cards with spaced repetition. Rate each card Again, Hard, Good or Easy, and the app shows when it comes back.
- Practice mode shuffles the cards of the decks you pick, so you can drill them any time, due or not.
- On a phone, tap the card to see the answer, then swipe right if you got it or left if you missed it. In a review, right is Good and left is Again.
- Cards can include images and Mermaid diagrams. Put `/` in a deck name to group decks, as in `Science / Chemistry basics`.
- Install the app on your phone or computer. After the first visit it works offline.
- Export a backup of your decks and progress from Settings, and restore it on any device.
- Private by design. Your cards stay on your device, and the app makes no requests to other sites.
